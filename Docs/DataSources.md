# RoobLeague Data Sources

This app build now uses real U.S. broomball references instead of placeholder data.

## Core sources used

- Woodbury Parks and Recreation TeamSideline: 2026 CoRec Broomball standings and schedule.
- City of Woodbury: M Health Fairview Sports Center ice arena details.
- Des Moines TeamSideline: 2026 Co-Ed Broomball standings, results, and playoff schedule.
- Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board: winter and spring broomball program details.
- City of Saint Paul: 2025-26 adult broomball season details.
- City of Minnetonka: Winter 2026 men's Thursday broomball league details.
- Minnesota Broomball Program: 2025-26 and 2026 tournament calendar.
- All Elite Broomball: national ecosystem context.

## Notes

- The app abbreviates one Des Moines team as `SSOI` to avoid surfacing the stronger official nickname directly in the UI.
- Some descriptive summaries in the UI are inferred from the official standings and schedules rather than quoted directly.

# RoobLeague Product Vision

## Positioning

RoobLeague should feel like the operating system for a broomball league, not just a scoreboard app. The product has to serve three audiences well:

- Players who need clarity, trust, and fast weekly logistics.
- Captains who manage attendance, roster shape, and communication.
- Organizers who control scheduling, compliance, standings, and public league presence.

## Core Product Pillars

1. Operational calm
The app should remove league chaos with excellent scheduling, timely updates, clear status states, and reliable match administration.

2. Community energy
Teams, stories, media, awards, and announcements should make the league feel alive between games.

3. Matchday confidence
A captain or organizer should be able to run a live game from an iPad without hunting for tools.

4. App Store quality
The experience should be accessible, quick, and visually distinctive enough to support screenshots, feature graphics, and future premium positioning.

## Full Product Scope

- Authentication with role-aware onboarding
- League creation and season setup
- Team registration and roster management
- Schedule builder with venue constraints
- Standings, stats, awards, and playoff brackets
- Match center with scorekeeping and event logging
- Push notifications and reminders
- Announcements, media, and community updates
- Subscription or league billing hooks
- Admin controls, moderation, and audit history

## Technical Direction

- SwiftUI-first iOS and iPadOS application
- Modular feature folders to keep product growth maintainable
- Shared design system for consistent components
- State container that can evolve into API-backed stores
- Future-ready path for widgets, Live Activities, and notifications
